Best Burger ever recipe

Description

This hamburger recipe uses ground beef and an easy crumb mixture. Nothing

beats a simple hamburger on a warm summer evening!

Enjoy a ciabatta, Kaiser or potato rolls

topped with your favorite condiments

Ingredients

  • 1 large egg
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1/2 cup fine dry bread crumbs

Steps

  1. Prehead an outdoor grill for high heat and lightly oil grate
  2. Whisk egg, salt and pepper together in a medium bowl
    3. \
  3. Add ground beef and bread crumbbs; mix with your hands
    or fork until well blended
